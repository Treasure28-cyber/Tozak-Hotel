# Tozak Hotel — creative direction

## The concept

**A little comfort. A little luxury. A place for you.**

Warm, understated hospitality for a real hotel in Ughelli. The website should feel considered and welcoming, with the actual property photographs, reassuring copy and an easy conversation with the hotel. Premium typography and space carry the design; there are no invented luxury credentials.

## Visual system

- Warm ivory `#FBF8F2`, deep teal `#103E43`, dark teal `#0A2E33`, pale sage `#E6EEEB`.
- Restrained decorative gold `#C39958`; amber action buttons `#E8A317` with dark text.
- Fraunces headings and Manrope body text, with system fallbacks and font display swap.
- Architectural arch/T monogram, refined serif wordmark, adaptable dark/light variants.
- Large left-aligned headings, quiet dividing lines, asymmetrical image layouts, compact eyebrow labels, restrained hover transitions.
- Mobile layouts prioritise readable headings, clear enquiry fields, 44px-or-larger primary controls and a fixed WhatsApp booking link.

## Page composition

1. **Home:** real pool photography, editorial headline and adjacent booking card; amenity strip; welcoming room/courtyard composition; room previews; pool/facilities split; gallery; direct-booking reassurance; FAQs; CTA.
2. **Rooms:** editorial introduction, four alternating photo/text room sections, direct enquiry buttons and a room-choice assistance strip.
3. **Facilities:** pool feature, six minimal amenity tiles and practical pre-arrival guidance.
4. **Gallery:** nine supplied photos, five filters and an accessible modal photo viewer.
5. **About:** hotel story, lounge photograph, comfort/value/welcome pillars and a clearly labelled illustrative map with real directions link.
6. **Contact:** contact details and WhatsApp link beside a full enquiry form, optional real map, FAQs and booking information.
